Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our counseling, consulting, and training services.

Who do you work with?

We work with children, adolescents, adults, educators, schools, and organizations. Our counseling services are available for individuals of all ages, and our consulting and training services are designed for schools, districts, and helping professionals.

What is trauma-informed counseling?

Trauma-informed counseling is an approach that recognizes the widespread impact of trauma and integrates knowledge about trauma into all aspects of service delivery. It emphasizes safety, trust, collaboration, choice, and empowerment, and seeks to actively avoid re-traumatization.

Do you offer school consulting?

Yes. We partner with schools and school districts to implement trauma-informed frameworks, build mental health support systems, design staff wellness programs, and provide targeted support for trauma-exposed student populations.

How can schools book training?

Schools and organizations can request training by completing our contact form or calling our office directly. We offer customized workshops and professional development sessions tailored to your team's specific needs.

Do you offer online courses?

Yes. We offer online continuing education courses and recorded workshops for mental health professionals. These self-paced learning opportunities cover trauma-informed practices, clinical interventions, and professional development topics.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We accept several major insurance plans including CareSource, BlueCross BlueShield, Anthem, Amerigroup, Aetna, United Healthcare, Tricare, Optum, and Cigna. Please contact us to verify your specific coverage.

Do you offer telehealth appointments?

Yes. We offer both in-person and virtual (telehealth) appointments for counseling services, providing flexibility and accessibility for our clients.

What areas do you specialize in?

Our clinical specializations include ADHD, anxiety, depression, trauma and PTSD, behavioral concerns, coping skills, anger management, adjustment disorders, mindfulness, school issues, self-esteem, social anxiety, and stress-related disorders.
