About the Founder

Meet LaMonica Green, LPC

A dedicated Licensed Professional Counselor, educator advocate, and doctoral student whose career has been shaped by a deep commitment to trauma-informed care and systemic mental health support.

Licensed Professional Counselor Doctoral Student in Counseling – Traumatology Trauma-Informed Specialist

Professional Background

Supporting Individuals. Sustaining Educators. Strengthening Systems.

LaMonica Green, LPC, is a Licensed Professional Counselor, clinical therapist, and doctoral student in counseling with a specialization in traumatology at Liberty University. She is the founder of Connect 4 Behavioral Solutions, a practice dedicated to promoting mental wellness, resilience, and trauma-informed systems within schools and communities.

LaMonica has worked in the field of education since 2007 and currently serves as a clinical therapist within a public school district, where she provides counseling services and collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to support the mental health needs of students and families. Licensed as a professional counselor since 2017 and in private practice since 2018, she provides counseling services to children, adolescents, and adults experiencing a range of mental health concerns.

Her clinical work includes supporting individuals who have experienced adverse childhood experiences (ACEs) as well as those navigating challenges such as anxiety, depression, ADHD, anger management concerns, and trauma-related stress. Through a trauma-informed and strengths-based approach, LaMonica works to help clients develop healthier coping strategies, improve emotional regulation, and build resilience.

In addition to her clinical work, LaMonica provides professional training and consultation through Connect 4 Behavioral Solutions, focusing on trauma-informed practices, educator wellness, and school-based mental health systems. Her work centers on helping schools and organizations better understand trauma, respond effectively to the needs of students, and support the well-being of helping professionals.

“Every child, every adult, and every educator deserves to feel safe, supported, and empowered to heal.” — LaMonica Green, LPC

Her doctoral research explores the implementation of trauma-informed practices in urban school settings and examines the impact of chronic stress and secondary trauma on providers who serve trauma-exposed populations. LaMonica is committed to advancing mental wellness by equipping individuals, educators, and organizations with the tools and knowledge needed to foster environments that support long-term healing and resilience.

Mission

Why We Do This Work

Mission: Connect 4 Behavioral Solutions is committed to promoting mental wellness and resilience by providing trauma-informed counseling, professional training, and consulting services that support individuals, educators, and organizations in creating healthier, more responsive environments for growth and healing.
